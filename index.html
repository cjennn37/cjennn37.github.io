A Decoding Algorithm for Length-Control Summarization Based on Directed Acyclic Transformers (presented in EMNLP 2024) [Paper] [Presentation]

This paper introduces SeqMAP, a non-autoregressive length-control decoding algorithm for the Directed Acyclic Transformer (DAT). SeqMAP leverages an approximative, dynamic-programming based maximization algorithm across a fixed canvas length to perform length-control summarization.

Tags: Natural Language Processing, Transformer Architectures, Sequence-to-Sequence, Non-Autoregressive Methods, Length Control Summarization, Data Science, Machine Learning, PyTorch, Predictive Modeling

Multi-Temporal Latent Diffusion Transformers for Cloud Removal in Remote Sensing Images (Presented in ISCM 2025) [Paper]

This paper proposes a transformer-based latent diffusion model architecture for the cloud removal task. Our architecture synthesizes key advantages offered by several distinct frameworks, combining the increased computational efficiency from latent-space processing, the localized, patch-granular precision from transformer architectures, and the high-fidelity restoration of the diffusion model generation process.

Tags: Computer Vision, Transformer Architectures, Data Science, Machine Learning, PyTorch, Predictive Modeling

Thesis: Uncertainty-Aware Value-Based Selective Planning Methods for Model-Based Reinforcement Learning [PDF]

My thesis research introduces novel selective planning frameworks that mitigate model inaccuracy in value-based reinforcement learning. After formalizing model inaccuracy via bootstrap target error, my work proposes two algorithms for robust planning updates, and characterizes their empirical strengths and trade-offs.

Tags: Reinforcement Learning, Model-Based Reinforcement Learning, Value-Based Methods, Selective Planning, Data Science, Machine Learning, Torch (Pytorch, libtorch), Predictive Modeling

Quantifying Opponent Behaviour: A Comparative Analysis of Lichess and Chess.com Games [Video to be released in November]

I conducted a behavioural analysis of opponent playstyles across Lichess and Chess.com using a dataset of hundreds of self-played blitz games. By extracting move-level meta-data from annotated PGN files, specifically move deltas and engine evaluations, I quantified metrics related to move accuracy, time-management heuristics, and blunder rates to profile platform-specific opponent behaviour.

Tags: Quantitative Analysis, Correlation Analysis, Hypothesis Testing, Bootstrap Methods, Data Analysis, Pandas, Power BI

Characterization of Tennis Archetypes Using Clustering Techniques [Video to be released in November]

Using point-by-point data from Jeff Sackmann's Match Charting Project, I applied unsupervised clustering and dimensionality reduction techniques to identify distinct playstyle archetypes across the ATP and WTA tours.
